About

Core Research Alliance (CRA) is an independent research and policy initiative supporting humanitarian and development actors through evidence-based analysis, monitoring, evaluation, and strategic research.

Services

Research & Policy Analysis

Evidence-based research and policy support for development and humanitarian programs.

Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning (MEAL)

Designing monitoring systems, evaluations, and learning frameworks to improve impact.

Humanitarian & Development Programming

Technical and operational support across multisectoral humanitarian and development initiatives.

Training & Capacity Development

Capacity strengthening, technical training, and knowledge transfer for organizations and communities.
